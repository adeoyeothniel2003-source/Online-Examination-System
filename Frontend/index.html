Online Examination System

A secure, modern examination platform for Obafemi Awolowo University. Sign in with your student credentials to take assigned examinations, or continue as an administrator to manage assessments.

Start Examination

How it works

Follow these four steps to complete your examination.

  1. Log in
    Use your student email and password to access your dashboard.
  2. Select an exam
    Choose from the examinations assigned to you.
  3. Answer questions
    Work through the questions within the allotted time.
  4. Submit
    Review your answers and submit before the timer runs out.
